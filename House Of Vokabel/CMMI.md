# The Capability Maturity Model Integration (CMMI)

a process improvement framework that helps organizations streamline
process improvement and encourage productive,
efficient behaviors that decrease risks in software,
product, and service development.

While it started in software engineering (developed by Carnegie Mellon University),
it’s now used across various industries to achieve:

Consistency: Ensuring that if a key employee leaves, the project doesn't fall apart.

Quality: Reducing defects and ensuring the final product meets customer requirements.

Predictability: Better at estimating costs, schedules, and resources.

Market Credibility: Many government contracts (especially DoD)
require companies to be at least CMMI Level 3.

## 5 Maturity Levels

| Level | Name                   | Focus                       | Key Characteristic                                                                                       |
|-------|------------------------|-----------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| 1     | Initial                | Success depends on "heroes" | Processes are unpredictable, poorly controlled, and reactive.                                            |
| 2     | Managed                | Project management          | Processes are planned, performed, measured, and controlled at the project level.                         |
| 3     | Defined                | Engineering & Standards     | Processes are well characterized and understood across the whole organization.                           |
| 4     | Quantitatively Managed | Data-driven control         | Processes are measured using statistical and other quantitative techniques.                              |
| 5     | Optimizing             | Continuous Improvement      | Focus is on improving process performance through incremental and innovative technological improvements. |
