# Access Control

Controlling who can do what.

Identification - Giving each device a digital fingerprint (who is who, and what is what!)

Keeping track of what is on the network

Authentication - Passwords, Security Tokens, Biometric Scans.

Confirming who is accessing and veryfing the user.

Authorisation - What the user is allowed to do. Permissions and rights, groups, roles

Principle of least privilige. - Give users the bear minimum access to do their job. No more and no less.
Minimising risk.

Accounting - Keeping track of what the authorised people are doing. Audit trail and logs
