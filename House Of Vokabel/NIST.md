# National Institute of Standards and Technology

The NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF) provides guidelines, best practices,
and standards to help organizations effectively manage cybersecurity risks.
Though originally designed for critical infrastructure,
it has since been adopted widely across public and private sectors.
Unlike other frameworks, NIST is flexible and voluntary, making it useful for tailoring
to fit an organization's size and risk profile.

- is a nonregulatory agency in the United States
- that establishes standards and best practices across science and technology.
- publishes a wide variety of guidance and best practices within the field of IT,
including cybersecurity.

Identify

- Figure out what you have got. Devices and potential risk,  Policies, Gap analysis,
- Strength and Weaknesses.

Protect

- Putting the safeguards in place.
- Building the walls and security systems
- Buying, developing and deploying processes and security devices.
- Installing firewalls
- Encrypting data and Access Control Measures.
- Security awareness training

Detect

- Making sure the controls are working
- Staying ahead of the attacks
- Ongoing persistent visibility
- Tools in place to analyse the data and security incidents

Respond

- What happens when you find a problem
- How a problem should be  identified, analysed, contain, get rid of threats
- Document and tested procedures for when an incident occurs.
- Isolating and containing the threat
- Communication on what is going on

Recovery

- Being able to bounce back, getting the data and systems back to normal
- Document for future incidents and continuously improving
