# E2EE

Part of Speech: Acronym Meaning/Definition: 🔒 End To End Encryption

🔒 End To End Encryption

End-to-End Encryption (E2EE) primarily ensures confidentiality because it
protects the data from being accessed or read by unauthorized parties during
transmission.

Helps in the [CIA Triad](../House%20Of%20Vokabel/CIA%20Triad.MD)

Confidentiality: E2EE ensures that only the intended sender and recipient can
access the content, keeping the information private.

Integrity: While E2EE's main focus is confidentiality, it also helps maintain
data integrity since encrypted data cannot be tampered with without detection.
