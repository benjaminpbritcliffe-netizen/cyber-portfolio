# Binary Heaps

Created: 18 April 2025 08:02
Is Completed: Yes

Binary heaps are a specialised binary tree used to efficiently implement priority queues.

Heaps are a powerful data structure that underpin many advanced algorithms in computer science. As a type of binary tree, heaps are structured to ensure that each parent node maintains a specific relationship with its children, known as the heap property. This property guarantees that the smallest or largest element can be retrieved in constant time, making heaps particularly effective for tasks requiring repeated priority-based operations. By examining how heaps are constructed and manipulated, you can gain insights into their role in improving the efficiency of priority queue implementations.
