# CERT: Computer Emergency Response Team

While often referred to as "Cyber" today,
the official term is Computer Emergency Response Team.

The Role: They act as the "firefighters" of the digital world.

They are responsible for responding to security incidents,
providing technical assistance,
and sharing threat intelligence to prevent future attacks.

Fun Fact:
The term "CERT" is actually a registered trademark
owned by Carnegie Mellon University,
which is why many organizations use the term CSIRT instead.
