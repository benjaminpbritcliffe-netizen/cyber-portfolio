CSIRT: Computer Security Incident Response Team

Your guess was close! It is actually Computer Security Incident Response Team.

The Role:
This is the formalized group within an organization that receives, reviews, and responds to computer security incident reports.

Difference: While a CERT often has a broader mission (like national coordination or research),
 a CSIRT is usually the specific internal team that handles the "boots on the ground" containment and recovery when a breach happens.
