# CIA Triad

Part of Speech: Acronym
Meaning/Definition: Confidentiality, Integrity, Availability

[CIA Triad](../Research%20Paper%20Planner%201763d034d9f780cb993bf4554600d5e7/Reading%20List%201763d034d9f781f7bbbfddf4a9dfb052/CIA%20Triad%201763d034d9f7801bb25bd78219ef0714.md) 

Confidentiality

**Confidentiality** means keeping data secret; that is, only authorized people can access or disclose the data.

Integrity

**Integrity** means ensuring that data is trustworthy and accurate by protecting it from unauthorized modification and destruction. 

Availability

**Availability** means ensuring timely and reliable access to and use of the data.
