# EPT/PPT

Part of Speech: Acronym
Meaning/Definition: Education/People, Process, Technology

Education/People

Needs to have people educated to ensure the first pillar of cybersecurity, such as training.

If the people are not trained or following Cyber Hygiene, then this could cause vulnerabilities.

Process

Need to have a simple and structured process for when anything goes wrong.

If the process is complex or has lots of deviations, then this could cause vulnerabilities.

Technology

The technology needs to be in place and not hinder people, if the technology hinders the people, they are more likely to disable or not use the software correctly.
