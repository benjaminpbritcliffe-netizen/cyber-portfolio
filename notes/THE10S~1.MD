# The 10 Stages Of  Cybersecurity

Status: To read

1. Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting and recovering data, networks, devices, and programs from digital threats.
2. Cybersecurity encompasses three main components: digital, human, and physical security measures.
3. Effective cybersecurity achieves the CIA triad's objectives: confidentiality, integrity, and availability.
4. Security controls are safeguards that detect, prevent, and minimize risks to physical and digital assets.
5. Organizations protect information assets through three essential elements: education, process, and technology.
6. Risk value equals the potential impact of a threat multiplied by its likelihood of occurrence.
7. Organizations manage risks through four approaches: acceptance, reduction, transference, or rejection.
8. Cybersecurity professionals need diverse skills—from strategic analysis and understanding evolving IT landscapes to maintaining vigilance in system monitoring.
9. International laws regulate both the use of computing technologies and the protection of their stored information.
10. Key ethical debates in cybersecurity center on retaliation, artificial intelligence, and government surveillance.
