## **Learning Objectives:**

At the end of this unit you will be able to:

1. Explain what an algorithm is. **[MLO1]**

> In lesson 1, you will explore what algorithms are, how they work, and why they are essential in programming and problem-solving. You'll learn how to design algorithms for efficiency, accuracy, and clarity, and see how they are applied in everyday technology—from sorting data to navigating maps. Understanding algorithms is key to mastering the logic behind computer programming and technology.
> 

Algorithms are step-by-step procedures or sets of rules designed to address specific challenges, whether it’s something as simple as finding the largest number in a list or as complex as encrypting online transactions. 

- **How would you explain the difference between correctness and efficiency in an algorithm? Can you think of a scenario where one might be prioritised over the other?**

- **Consider the sorting problem. How might the choice of sorting algorithm change based on the size or type of data you are working with?**

- **How do you think the concept of generalisation applies to algorithms outside of sorting? Can you identify other tasks in everyday software applications where generalisation is crucial?**
