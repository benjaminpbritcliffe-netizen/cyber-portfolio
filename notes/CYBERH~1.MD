# Cyber Hygiene

Date: 11 January 2025
Tags: Definition

What are some examples of cyber hygiene practices?

Use strong passwords: Use unique passwords for each account and system, and change them regularly.

 Update software: Keep your operating system, apps, and web browsers up to date Use antivirus and firewall software: Install antivirus software and use firewalls to stop unauthorized access.

 Be careful online: Avoid suspicious links and unknown files, and protect sensitive information on social media and email

 Use secure connections: Use secure internet connections 

Back up data: Regularly back up all of your data Enable device encryption: Enable screen lock and device encryption Use biometric authentication: Use biometric authentication to log in to your devices
