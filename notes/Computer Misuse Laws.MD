# Computer Misuse Laws

Laws prohibit unauthorised or umapproved access or use of a computing device.

hijacking computers through technical material or forcing access is banned.

Can catch people circumventing broken controls such as authentication or placing a fake login screen on a website to steal credentials and spying on someones account.

Preventing others from legitimate us such as DDos/DOS attacks.  Also degrading the QOS for others

Aiding a criminal or designing malware. Such as being an accompice and/or writing malicious software.

Unauthorised data alteration such as deleting or blocking personal data or altering data to harm a person and/or organisation.

Prohibited software such as distributing software designed to commit cyber crimes, such as viruses, malware and hacking tools.

Cyberstalking such as bullying, intimidation or stalking others.
