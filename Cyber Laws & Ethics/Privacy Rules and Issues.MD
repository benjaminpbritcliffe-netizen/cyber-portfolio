# Privacy Rules & Issues

Status: In progress

[CIA Triad](../House%20Of%20Vokabel/CIA%20Triad.MD)

A privacy breach can be classed as technically not giving the user the right for
privacy, such as: If you don’t accept or enable location sharing or certain
sections of the T&cs, then you can not use their product. Privacy is all about
having the right to share (or not share) certain aspects to either the company
or other users.

Examples of privacy rules being potentially broken.

Location Services being used to manipulate the behaviour of the user, such as
sending coupons etc to entice the user to make a purchase or a decision that was
influenced by an external source.

A business using data to influence future purchases, using previous orders as
leverage.

A vehicle company sharing data to insurance brokers about the drivers habits
such as breaking, speeding etc and using this data to influence the price of Car
Insurance.

Using an app to track the users behaviour or location for malicious (and even
non-malicious) activities.

Sharing data unintentionally via social media either to Friends/Family or the
public.

A business sharing/selling your data to other businesses, sometimes not even
industry related!
