# Misconceptions Of Cyber Security

Misconception #1 -

Workers in Cybersecurity come from an IT Background

Workers in cybersecurity all come from different backgrounds, Cybersecurity also
requires a range of different skills to form a team.

Misconception #2 -

All hackers are criminals

Hackers is often used as a bad term and also misconstrued as “illegal”. While
there is criminals and criminal hackers, there are many other types of hackers.

🕵️Black Hat

Grey Hat

White Hat

Misconception #3 -

Cybersecurity is something I can not do

This goes back to misconception #1. Many skill sets are needed in cyber
security, and isn’t just technical strength.

Misconception 4 - I’m too young or too old for the cybersecurity industry.

Cybersecurity requires both old and young people to perform well as a team. It
requires experience and knowledge, as well as new thinking and new methods as
well as experienced and well known methods too.
