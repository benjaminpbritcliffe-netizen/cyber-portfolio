PPT/EPT = People/Education, Process Technology

The three key elements of Cyber Security are:

- Education/People

The first pillar of cybersecurity is people. This includes everyone from IT
staff to end-users. People are often seen as the weakest link in cybersecurity,
but they can become the strongest defence with proper training and awareness.

Education and awareness are effective ways to improve cybersecurity

Think of holding training sessions, raise awareness of cybersecurity challenges.
Build a human firewall for when technology misses.

- Process

The second pillar is processes. These are the policies, procedures, and
protocols that govern how an organisation manages and protects its information.
Effective processes ensure a structured approach to handling data and responding
to incidents.

Having streamlined processes can help with cybersecurity .

📢

implement a process on what do when a cyber attack of any kind occurs or how to
ensure you keep an area secure.

Processes include everything from access control policies to incident response
plans.

When creating non-security processes, keep cybersecurity in mind.

📢

A lengthy process can hinder the amount of people reporting an issue or using
the process.

📢

A good process with be clear and as easy as possible, what to do at every stage.
The process stages should be obvious, avoid ambiguity.

Make sure the processes are accessible and well known.

Make sure the processes are consistent and do not contradict. if a process has
alot of deviations and contradictions, this can cause confusion and
cyberattackers will exploit inconsistency.

- Technology

Technology is all the organisations infrastructure.

Technology is the third pillar. This encompasses the tools and solutions used to
protect an organisation’s digital assets. Technology includes firewalls,
antivirus software, encryption, and intrusion detection systems, among others.

While technology is essential, it must be used in conjunction with the other two
pillars to be effective. For example, deploying advanced threat detection
systems can help identify and mitigate threats in real-time. However, without
trained personnel to manage these systems and processes to guide their use, the
technology alone cannot provide comprehensive security.

Good uses of technology will solve problems without creating new ones for users.

📢

Good technical security is device management software, if implemented correctly
then the security would be passive and not hinder the user. If implemented
poorly, users may try to disable the software entirely creating a risk to the
system.

Organisations need to educate their users on the reasons for introducing certain
technology to ensure it is used effectively.
