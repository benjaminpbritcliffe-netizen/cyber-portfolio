# Have I Been Pwned (HIBP)

**Have I Been Pwned** checks if an email address has appeared in a leaked data breach.

## Why It Matters

- Finding your email in leaked data means private info has been exposed.
- Many users reuse passwords → one breach can compromise multiple accounts.
- Even if stored encrypted, weak passwords can often be cracked.
