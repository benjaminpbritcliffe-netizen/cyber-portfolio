# Censys

At first glance, **Censys** looks similar to Shodan.  

## Key Difference
- **Shodan** → Focuses on devices and systems (servers, routers, webcams, IoT).  
- **Censys** → Focuses on Internet-connected hosts, websites, certificates, and assets.  

## Use Cases
- Enumerating domains in use  
- Auditing open ports and services  
- Discovering rogue assets within a network
