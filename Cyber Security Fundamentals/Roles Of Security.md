# Security Of Roles

## CISO

Chief Information Security Officer -
Developing overseeing the security procedures and teams.
Documenting and maintaining Access Control Policies.
The strategic leader

## CIO

Chief Information Officer -

Reponsible for the IT Infrastructure. The networking, hardware etc.

## CTO

Chief Technology Officer -

External and Product itself, the development of new products and services.

## CEO

Chief Executive Officer -

The business bosss. Who needs to have an eye on cyber security from top down.

## SOC

Security Operations Centre -

Managing the security systems, the watch dogs.
Monitoring and analysing the security events.

## NOC

Network Operations Centre -

Keeping the network and operations smoothly.
Stability and Efficency

## Helpdesk

First point of contact, handling the troubleshooting and ticket resolution.
Small companies can use the Helpdesk to do NOC and SOC Functionality.

## MSP

Managed Service Providers -

Outside organisation to handle IT functions.
Outsourcing some IT Infratructure/Services.
