
# Private IP Address Ranges (RFC 1918)

they are:

10.0.0.0 (Class A: Used for massive corporate networks, allows millions of hosts)

172.16.0.0 (Class B: Used for medium to large networks)

192.168.0.0 (Class C: Used for small home or office networks)

## Common Default Router Gateways

Most home routers use the very first usable IP address in their subnet
as their initial setup address:

192.168.1.1 (Linksys, ASUS, Netgear, TP-Link)

192.168.0.1 (D-Link, Netgear)

10.0.0.1 (Xfinity, Apple, Cisco)
